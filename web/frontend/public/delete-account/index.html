Delete your RiddleVerse account

For the RiddleVerse Android app (com.kreativekoala.riddleverse) and puzzleverseai.com. Last updated November 10, 2026.

How to request deletion

  1. Email puzzleverseai@gmail.com with the subject Delete my RiddleVerse account.
  2. Send it from the email address you signed in with, or tell us that address and your display name. We may reply with one question to confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and its data and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

There is no in-app delete button yet, so deletion is by email request.

What we delete

  • Your profile and sign-in record.
  • Your progress, scores, streaks, badges, coin balance and history, and leaderboard entries.
  • Your preferred topics, notification token and notification settings.
  • Your usage events, feedback, ratings and generation history.
  • Games and puzzle sets you created.

What may remain

  • Purchase and tax records that the law requires us to keep, and records of Google Play transactions held by Google.
  • Copies in routine backups, which are overwritten on the backups' normal schedule.
  • Data that Google, advertising partners and other providers hold under their own policies (see the Privacy Policy). You can manage the advertising ID in Android Settings, under Google, then Ads.

Just want to stop?

Uninstalling the app stops all collection from the device but does not delete data we already hold. To stop reminder notifications only, turn them off in Android Settings for RiddleVerse.
